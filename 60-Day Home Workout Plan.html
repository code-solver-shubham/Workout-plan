🗓 Weekly Plan (Mon–Sat, Sun rest)

Every exercise: 4 sets × 12–15 reps, rest 45–60 sec. Push-ups & planks use the ladder below. Warm-up: 2 min march + arm circles + 10 bodyweight squats.

🎯 Full Push-up Ladder

PhaseDaysWhat to do
11–14Half push-ups 4×12 with slow 3-sec lowering. Chest to ~fist height from floor.
215–28Hands on a bed/table edge (incline) 4×12–15, then lower the surface each week.
329–42Negatives: start full plank, lower in 5 sec, knees up. 4×6–8 + incline push-ups 2×15.
443–56Full push-ups: 4 sets of 1–3 clean reps (rest 60s), add 1 rep/week. Rest with negatives.
Test57–60Max full push-ups in one set. Target 5–10! 🎉

Rule: only count a rep if body is straight (no sagging hips), chest near floor, elbows ~45°.

⬆️ Progressive Overload (How to add)

  • Double progression: Do all 4 sets at 12 → keep same weight and add reps until 15 on all 4 sets. Then make it harder (below) and drop back to 12.
  • Days 1–14: 4×12, slow tempo (3 sec down, 1 sec up). Master form.
  • Days 15–28: 4×15 same tempo. Rest shortened 60s → 45s.
  • Days 29–42: Make it harder with one dumbbell: single-leg versions, 1-sec pause at the bottom, 4×12.
  • Days 43–56: Hold dumbbell farther from body / higher reps (to 15), slower 4-sec lowering, 4×12→15.
  • Days 57–60: Test week: max reps with clean form. Compare with Day 1.
  • One dumbbell trick: make it heavier by using one arm/leg at a time, adding pauses, and adding a backpack with books (2–5 kg) for squats, lunges and bridges.
  • Log reps in a notebook. If last set feels like 2 reps left in the tank, you're in the sweet spot.
